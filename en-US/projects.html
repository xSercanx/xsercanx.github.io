Computer simulations in my bachelor's degree program in Physics
2D lattice
2D clusters
2D cluster
A percolation problem on a two- and three- dimensional lattice was studied in order to discover the critical threshold of percolation under the condition of a preferred direction with the usage of clusters or connecting vertices. The simulations were written in C++. For analysis purposes or further processing python and gnuplot were used. The thesis itself was written in LaTeX.
My interest in how things in the world works lead me to start studying physics and at some point or points I came into contact with programming. The concept of creating things throught programming amazed me and thus I finished my bachelor's degree in physics in the computational department of physics. This interest in programming and generally also computer science mixed with media has currently swayed me to go into media and computer science and learn more about it.
